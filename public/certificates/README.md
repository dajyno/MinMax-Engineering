# Certificates Upload Directory

Upload your scanned certificate files (JPG, PNG, WebP, or SVG) directly into this directory:
`/public/certificates/`

### Recommended Filenames:
- `cac-certificate.jpg` (or `.png` / `.svg`) - Corporate Affairs Commission RC1459932
- `nuprc-permit.jpg` - Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission
- `nmdpra-permit.jpg` - Nigerian Midstream & Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority
- `nogic-jqs.jpg` - Nigerian Content Development & Monitoring Board (NCDMB)
- `firs-tax-clearance.jpg` - Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)
- `scuml-efcc.jpg` - SCUML / EFCC Anti-Money Laundering Compliance
- `itf-certificate.jpg` - Industrial Training Fund Compliance Certificate
- `nsitf-certificate.jpg` - Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund Certificate

The About Page will automatically render these certificates in the Compliance Archive grid with full-resolution click-to-enlarge lightbox viewing.
